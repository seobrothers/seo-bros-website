How we will know it is working

You will see it in the same lead sheet you already use. Any lead that clicks through from these sites arrives tagged with the referring site automatically, the same way organic and PPC leads are tagged today.

Alongside that: rank tracking on every target keyword above, indexing and impression data from Search Console, and a monthly spot check of what ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity answer when asked for the best commercial cleaner in the test market.
