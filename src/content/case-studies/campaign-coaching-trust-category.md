Business coaching is a trust-driven, high-consideration purchase. Buyers research extensively, compare multiple coaches, and often follow content for months before reaching out. The Los Angeles market is also one of the most competitive coaching markets in the country.

Ranking in this category is less about beating competitors on a single search and more about owning enough surface area in the buyer's research process that "the coach who keeps showing up" becomes the obvious choice.

## Audit findings

[Specific competitor positions redacted at the partner's request.]

22 monthly organic sessions at launch. No location-specific landing pages for Los Angeles coaching searches. Service pages lacked the depth that high-consideration buyers actually read before committing to a discovery call.

## What we shipped

Months one and two were technical and a pass on the core service pages, rewriting them for the depth the category requires. Months three through five were authority content and local optimization, building the resource library a serious coaching buyer reads through before booking. Months six and seven were where the rankings matured, with the two highest traffic months arriving at the very end of the campaign.

## Outcome

Traffic moved from 22 monthly organic sessions to 89, a 305% lift over seven months, with growth still accelerating at the campaign close. Several "1-on-1 business coaching" and "team coaching" terms reached #1, with broader category terms like "group coaching los angeles" climbing 38 positions.

Tracked GA4 leads grew from zero in month one to 15 in month six, totaling 42 across the campaign. The pattern matters more than the absolute numbers: in trust categories, content authority compounds slowly then accelerates. We expect month-twelve traffic to be materially higher than month-seven.
