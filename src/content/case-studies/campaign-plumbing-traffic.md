The campaign began with a familiar problem: a strong local brand with reputation and reviews to spare, but a website that couldn't rank outside its single home city.

## Audit findings

[Several specifics redacted at the partner's request.]

The site had been built for one location and then duplicated awkwardly across service areas, leaving thin content, duplicate meta, and a muddled internal link graph.

## What we shipped

- A programmatic city-page architecture covering every service area, generated from a single content template with locally-relevant variables.
- LocalBusiness and Service schema across the property — properly nested, not just bolted on.
- An internal linking restructure that funneled equity from the homepage and blog content to the highest-converting service pages.
- A citation cleanup pass to fix NAP inconsistencies that had accumulated over years.

## Outcome

By month nine, qualified organic traffic had more than tripled and booked service calls were up 184% year-over-year. The site now ranks in the top three for 47 priority local terms.
