When this partner first reached out, they were a thriving boutique agency with a steady client base and a strong reputation in their niche. The catch: every SEO opportunity that hit their pipeline turned into a hiring conversation they didn't want to have.

## The challenge

[Some details redacted at the partner's request.]

They had three options on the table — hire a senior SEO in-house, push SEO leads to a referral partner, or find a white-label team. The first two had been tried already, with predictable results.

## The approach

We embedded as their dedicated SEO bench. A single strategist owned the relationship, joined client calls when needed, and ran a unified reporting cadence under the agency's brand.

Onboarding took two weeks. By month three, they'd added four new SEO retainers without making a single hire.

## The result

Eighteen months in, organic recurring revenue is 4× what it was at the start of the partnership. More importantly, the agency now leads with SEO in pitches — a flip from where they started.
