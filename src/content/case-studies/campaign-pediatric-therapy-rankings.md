Pediatric therapy and applied behavior analysis services involve a long, anxiety-loaded consideration cycle. Parents research a provider extensively, often during a window of acute need, and they search for very specific service terms.

The practice was based in Western Colorado with a service area extending across the broader regional valley. At launch, core ABA and behavioral health terms were ranking outside the top 70, which is functionally invisible during the few weeks a parent is actively comparing providers.

## Audit findings

[Specific cities and competitor positions redacted at the partner's request.]

Core ABA terms buried outside position 70. No dedicated service pages for the discrete therapy types: ABA, speech, occupational, physical, and behavioral. Geographic targeting was limited to the immediate community.

## What we shipped

Months one and two were technical foundation work and a service page rebuild, with each therapy type getting its own page written for the parent who already knows what they need. Months three and four were content depth, E-E-A-T signals (provider authority, compliance, evaluation processes), and link work. Months five and six were the inflection point, with the final month delivering 470 sessions, the campaign high.

## Outcome

Traffic moved from 93 monthly organic sessions to 470, a 405% lift over six months. Ten priority terms climbed dramatically, including "applied behavior analysis [city]" from #79 to #3 and several autism-evaluation terms reaching #1.

Tracked GA4 leads grew from 3 a month at launch to 33 in the best month, totaling 93 across the campaign. Healthcare categories with long consideration cycles tend to plateau early and then re-accelerate as authority compounds. Month-six traffic at the campaign high is the signal we look for.
