Four days, Monday to Thursday, and then the way we ship changed. The client portal's home became the campaign plan, monitoring copy was rewritten in plain language, and on Thursday every change started going through a reviewed pull request with automated checks.

## For our partners

The client portal opens on the campaign plan. A monthly process strip runs along the top with deliverable tiles and this month's progress, then one section per phase with a checklist of what is done, in progress and pending: the brief, competitors, the goal, Google accounts, keyword research and tracking, gap analysis, keyword mapping, work plans. Phases are clickable tabs, and the ongoing view is one work list. The public share portal mirrors the same view. The process itself is a catalog of cards layered platform to partner to campaign, so a partner's standard process applies everywhere and a single campaign can differ, edited beside Scope without touching any markdown.

Monitoring reads differently. Check descriptions lead with the change they catch, in plain language, without internal policy detail. Websites display as their full address, host and folder, everywhere. Monitored pages match on URL regardless of a trailing slash. The noindex alert names the pages. Alert emails batch per evaluation pass instead of arriving one at a time.

Partners who write their own content can run a campaign in brief-only mode: we plan and brief, their writers produce the pieces, and the plan reflects that honestly instead of stamping our work as done. Content reviews can cancel an open approval request to keep editing. Team mentions from the old tool carried over into a Mentions tab in the inbox. Portal deliverables read as counted lines, and the plan ledger dropped its done counters and summary paragraph.

## Behind the scenes

Agent probation: an agent under review has its tasks and plans held until staff release them, invisible to partner and client until then. Website edits obey page physics, one active edit per page and a cooldown after completion, enforced whether the agent or a human creates the task. Long runs can park between turns and continue as the same session, so a big job is not cut off by a runtime limit, with a cost hold that staff release. Campaign phase starts at activation rather than at the first agent run. Page expansions are tasks now, not plan items; plans hold new content. The experiments concept was retired.

On Thursday the platform moved to a pull-request workflow: automated checks on every change, database changes that apply when a change lands, and a written doctrine of short-lived branches with a hold for review on schema, money and auth changes.
