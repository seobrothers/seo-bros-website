A Thursday split between the Sessions and Keywords pages and a long pass on how agents read a website.

## For our partners

Every row on the Scheduled sessions card now says what it runs on for this campaign ("Monthly on the 15th", "Every other Tuesday", "On request") and when it runs next, in your clock. Sessions can now run every other week, quarterly, twice yearly and yearly.

Reports gain two more switches under Website and Business Profile, after a partner reviewing the report sent a screenshot: hide the six-month trend chart itself, or hide only the dashed year-prior line on it.

On the Keywords page, a rank cell names its state instead of collapsing everything into Not in top 100: checked with a date, an older check with its date, awaiting first check, tracking not started, tracking paused. Tasks our side cancelled as triage no longer appear in your task list or activity; a cancel made by your own team still does.

The WordPress Plugin tab gains a share link that always serves your current branded build, and the plugin now updates from a neutral service address that carries no agency's name.

## Behind the scenes

Our site-wide optimization agent had been recommending caching and hosting work built on the wrong number. The crawler's response time was how long the whole page took to arrive, not how long the server took to answer. The crawler now records the server's answer time only when it reached the server directly, the speed check grades it in bands, and it no longer counts toward the technical score. Agents also now know what a connected site actually runs: WordPress and PHP versions, theme, SEO plugin, caching.

Agents also read pages properly now. When an agent opens a page it gets an outline first: the headings, every link with its text and where it sits on the page, images with their alt text, canonical, robots and schema types. And an agent cannot create a task about a page it has not opened live in that sitting; one had ordered links from two posts that already carried them, working from an earlier crawl. Search Console auto-linking picks the property that covers exactly the campaign's site, so franchise campaigns on a shared domain no longer land on another territory's property.
