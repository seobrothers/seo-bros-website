Three changes are the story today: the platform on your own domain, a Rules tab for every campaign, and featured images on content.

## For our partners

Partners with a live white-label domain can now sign in and use the whole platform there, not only their clients' portal. Connecting Google or your CRM from your own domain works too: the connection is made under your own brand and returns you to your domain when it finishes.

Every campaign has a Rules tab, beside Scope. A rule is a standing directive for the campaign (never change the pricing page, always link to the booking form), added and edited by your managers. Agents read the rules and can propose one, never edit or delete one.

Content pieces now have a featured image: drop a file or paste a URL in the editor's sidebar, write the alt text, and it shows above the piece on review pages and publishes as the featured image on WordPress, Ghost and Shopify.

Clicking Approve on a content piece while your own comments or unsent notes are still waiting on it asks whether to go back or approve as is. Our publishers also see the review thread on the task before they publish.

Smaller: a Business Profile post that cannot publish because Google is not connected tells you, and publishes on its own once it is. Guest posts and Business Profile posts no longer wait a fixed number of days between placements; the plan's due dates set the pace. Your approval rules now apply to work our own team files, not only the agents'.

## Behind the scenes

Feedback on a written piece has a reacting agent: it reads the draft, folds your notes into the brief, rewrites and resubmits, and answers a question in the thread. Briefs for approved pieces now come from dedicated brief-writer agents before the writing starts.

Agents can read a whole crawl: the page table, broken links with every page linking to them, the sitemap reconciled against the crawl, and live Search Console. Every arbitrary cap on what an agent can read is gone.

Admins got two more Operations views: Data Feeds, which flags a campaign whose connections need attention before any work depends on them, and a single queue of every item across the book that is waiting on a person, so nothing has to be hunted for campaign by campaign.
