Fifty-one changes in a day. Three of them are the story.

## For our partners

Partners with a live white-label domain can now sign in and use the whole platform there, not only their clients' portal. Connecting Google or your CRM from your own domain works too: the connection is made through your brand host and returns you to your domain when it finishes.

Every campaign has a Rules tab, beside Scope. A rule is a standing directive for the campaign (never change the pricing page, always link to the booking form), added and edited by your managers. Agents read the rules and can propose one, never edit or delete one.

Content pieces now have a featured image: drop a file or paste a URL in the editor's sidebar, write the alt text, and it shows above the piece on review pages and publishes as the featured image on WordPress, Ghost and Shopify.

If you click Approve on a content piece while your own comments or unsent notes are still waiting on it, a confirm asks whether to go back or approve as is. One partner's review comments were missed this way and the piece went out unchanged; that is closed, and our publishers now see the review thread on the task before they publish.

Smaller: a Business Profile post that cannot publish because Google is not connected tells you, and publishes on its own once it is. Guest posts and Business Profile posts no longer wait a fixed number of days between placements; the plan's due dates set the pace. Your approval rules now apply to work our own team files, not only the agents'.

## Behind the scenes

Feedback on a written piece has a reacting agent: it reads the draft, folds your notes into the brief, rewrites and resubmits, and answers a question in the thread. Briefs for approved pieces now come from dedicated brief-writer agents before the writing starts.

Agents can query a whole crawl: the page table, broken links with every page linking to them, the sitemap reconciled against the crawl, and live Search Console. Every arbitrary cap on what an agent can read is gone; the only limits left are physical ones.

Admins got two more Operations views: Data Feeds, which flags a campaign whose connections put promised work at risk before the tasks block, and Stuck Work, one queue of everything across the book that stopped moving.
