A day for the white label: the plugin now looks like yours, and the approval flow gets a way to move on when a client does not answer.

## For our partners

The WordPress connector is white-label all the way down. Settings has a new WordPress Plugin tab: set the plugin's name, author, website and support email, preview how it reads on a site's Plugins screen, and download your build. Every connected site runs your build, and the account it signs in with carries your team's name.

![The WordPress Plugin settings tab: download your build, and a preview of how it reads on a site's Plugins screen](/images/updates/2026-09-09/wordpress-plugin-tab.png)

Written Content approvals get an optional fallback. Partners who send content to clients for approval report that clients sit on reviews for months. Turn it on in Settings > Approvals with a number of days, and a client can override or opt out on its own Approval rules. The platform asks first; if the client does not answer inside the window it approves the piece as is and proceeds. The clock starts when the review was requested, a send-back resets it, only reviews opened after you turn it on qualify, and a reminder goes out two days before the window closes. Off everywhere by default.

Reports: a Website toggle lets a client with Analytics linked get a keyword-only report, and each KPI section has its own switches for the previous-period and year-over-year comparisons. A partner toggled Conversions off and on during a client call and could not tell whether it worked, so anything hidden from the client's report is now an amber banner listing everything hidden.

![A client's Reporting page with an amber banner listing what is hidden from the client's report](/images/updates/2026-09-09/hidden-from-report-banner.png)

A content piece closed while its review was still out reads Pending approval on the planning page, not Published. Every filtered table works at phone width: the search stays inline and the dropdowns open in a Filters sheet, with an active-filter count and a Clear button.

## Behind the scenes

An agent raised an alarm that the rank tracker had returned no data two days after keywords were added. They had been added that morning; the agent had been started by hand four times within half an hour and treated each start as a new day. Every ranking an agent reads now carries the current time, when the keyword was registered, when it was last checked and how often it is checked, and every session opens with the time of day. Escalations name the agent that raised them.
