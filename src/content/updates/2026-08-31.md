The last day of August was small. Two things for partners, and a sweep of the link pipeline underneath.

## For our partners

Approving a task, or sending it back with changes, is open to every member of your team. Plan approvals and content reviews already were; task decisions had been limited to owners and admins. Approval rules and workspace approval defaults stay with managers.

Content review links sent from the old system, the ones a client may still have in an inbox, now land on the platform's approval page for that piece. A handful of client approvals had been given through those old links since the move and were carried over by hand; the links themselves now come here.

## Behind the scenes

Agents can now see the standalone technical check series the auditor runs (soft 404s, missing pages that redirect, www and https resolution, sitemap discovery, robots, safe browsing, indexed count), including passing state and past runs, and can re-probe a site on demand to verify their own fix rather than waiting for the next scheduled run.

Four defects found during a link planning sweep are closed at the cause. One manual fire produces one run, not two overlapping ones. The agents that write a guest post's topic and article can no longer close the task mid-pipeline; the pipeline closes it at publish. A placement that named the same target URL twice could never save; those dedupe on the way in. And a campaign with no link network selection routes to classification instead of stalling on an empty pool.
