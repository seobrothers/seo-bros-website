Monday was a reporting day, with a run of campaign-details follow-ups alongside.

## For our partners

The client report can now be viewed and downloaded for a calendar quarter as well as a month, as it could in the old Portal. The period picker has a Months and Quarters toggle with a year stepper; only finished quarters are offered. Comparisons follow the period: quarter over quarter and against the same quarter last year, keyword movement from the start of the quarter to its end. It works everywhere the report renders, including the client portal, the public share link and the PDF. Scheduled monthly sends are unchanged.

The report has a Work Delivered section: the content pieces and link placements completed in the period, each linking to its live URL where we hold the publish receipt. It is on by default, can be hidden in the report settings, and drops out on its own when a period had neither.

On campaign details, a Define campaign profile step joins the checklist, with a Detect from website button that classifies the client for you, and any phase tab offers a confirm-gated Move to this phase. Competitive gap reviews now live where the rest of monitoring does: settings and a Run review now button on the Monitoring page, results under a Competitive Gap tab on Audits. The standalone page is retired. New reviews no longer include the AI visibility comparison, since the monthly AI visibility snapshot already measures the client against competitors across all four assistants.

Content pieces approved in the old system, or cleared by your trust settings without a review, now read Approved instead of Draft. Submit for approval hides on approved and pending pieces and returns as Resubmit only when a newer version has been saved. Page monitoring gains an alert for a monitored page whose HTML balloons between audits, critical when it nears the size at which Google stops indexing.

## Behind the scenes

Long agent sessions on big campaigns now finish. Replies stream in as they are generated rather than arriving whole, which was timing out on the largest campaigns; eleven runs had died that way in two weeks. Agents can also wake when a crawl completes, with conditions on the crawl's own numbers, and they arrive with the keyword map and their own open tasks already in front of them. Every manual run records who fired it.
