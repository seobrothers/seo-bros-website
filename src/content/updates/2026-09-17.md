Thursday split between the settings pages partners live in and how campaign knowledge is stored.

## For our partners

Each member row on Settings > Team now shows how many clients that person is assigned to. Click it and a searchable checklist of your active clients opens, so moving a teammate onto or off several clients is one save instead of a visit to every client's Team tab.

![The client assignments dialog opened from a member's row on the Team page: a search field and a checklist of active clients](/images/updates/2026-09-17/team-client-assignments.png)

Integrations opens with a connected-data table: one row per client, with Search Console, Analytics, Business Profile and Ads, naming the property and the Google account each rides on. The page could not answer the question a partner opens it for, is my clients' data flowing, when the accounts were ours; company access accounts now show read-only. The Vault is one row per campaign with its CMS and access state (connected, login on file, connection broken, none), and Webhooks is one row per event, edited one at a time, with a status.

![The Integrations page opening with a Connected data table, one row per client with Search Console, Analytics and Business Profile columns](/images/updates/2026-09-17/integrations-connected-data.png)

Every rule is now a Rule, never broken, or Guidance, lean this way and say why when you do not. Notes & feedback is open to every partner; a note the review finds nothing in closes itself, and a note that changed nothing can be deleted. Review threads show every comment in full.

## Behind the scenes

A strategist's comment had been written into a campaign's shared memory with "keep flagging this every time until removed", and every agent on that campaign obeyed it, including ones with no business acting on it. Memory was shared by every agent and never expired, and most of it had become instructions to whichever agent came next.

What agents keep is now typed. Facts are dated, sourced rows about the business and the site, and a fact a person states cannot be corrected by an agent. Each agent has its own notebook in place of a daily handoff log. Events record what changed and why. The existing memory was sorted into those three, the results read, and the sorting rules corrected twice: strategy lifted from an old goal never becomes guidance, and a fact never restates something the platform already holds.

Connected sites on a WordPress host that only allows plugin updates from inside its admin now take connector updates that way. Agents can be scoped to run everywhere except named partners or campaigns.
