A Sunday. Nothing moved on partner screens; the work was all underneath.

## For our partners

Nothing moved on the surface today; the work was all underneath.

## Behind the scenes

1. Connector 2.2.0 and 2.3.0 were built inside real on-page tasks, one limitation at a time.
2. The connector installer now handles more of what real hosts throw at it, and refuses to install when the login on file points at a different site than the client's (a staging copy).
3. The staff task queue no longer lists tasks with a pending approval request, in the open queue or on Upcoming. Pending Approvals is where those are followed up, and its plan rows now show how many pieces each plan carries.

### 1. Connector 2.2.0 and 2.3.0

Link insertion takes a context phrase, so when an anchor repeats on a page the agent names the sentence instead of widening the anchor. The page outline reports children counts, device visibility and background images, and every element can be named exactly, so a block can be cloned with its design, a heading's level changed with its look pinned, and a top-level section moved, on Elementor, the block editor, classic pages, Divi and WPBakery. Bench-checked before release.

### 2. The installer

It gets through security check pages that clear themselves in a browser, admin email prompts, custom two-factor screens, passwordless login switches, and maintenance mode after an upload. Installs now happen case by case as tasks need them.
