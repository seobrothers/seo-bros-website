Three changes worth telling today: asks, notes, and a tidier client workspace.

## For our partners

Until today an agent had two outputs, a task or silence. Neither fit a question like whether a client would move off a slow host. An ask is the third output: one question, optional proposed answers, and a follow-up as its own thread. The sidebar entry is now Asks & Approvals, open asks lead your daily or weekly digest, a task waiting on an answer shows as exactly that on the client's list, and answering wakes the agent that asked. Our team and the agents can both ask.

Notes & feedback replaces Comments in the sidebar, and the notes you send are read as soon as they land. Each note becomes a short list of proposed changes: a rule to add, something to remember, campaign context, or work for the team. Nothing changes until someone accepts, and managers can switch on applying automatically. You can upload a call transcript in place of a note, and a note that no longer applies can be withdrawn, with what it changed undone.

The client workspace has fewer doors. Planning & Tasks, Reporting & Activity, and Keywords & Mapping each hold tabs that used to be separate items; Configuration split into Campaign and Client; workspace settings have a grouped side nav. Keywords added by hand now pull Google search volume, research needs a location, and the same term tracked in two locations is two keywords.

Alerts are handled by our team for now and no longer appear in the partner workspace. The setting asking whether approved changes may be applied on a connected site is gone; connecting the site is the answer. Discovery reports strike word-order and spelling variants Google reports at the same volume and count the demand once.

## Behind the scenes

Agents now see every page at desktop and phone width before filing a visible change, and a blocked render is recognised as blocked rather than mistaken for the page. The monthly planners fire only where their deliverable is in scope.

The note review was tuned over dozens of dry runs against real notes and invented scenarios before it read a single live note. The team can also send an email thread or a call to a client's notes straight from our inbox.
