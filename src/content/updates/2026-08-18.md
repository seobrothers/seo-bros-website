Two threads today: what happens when a client pauses, and how fast Google data arrives when one starts.

## For our partners

Inactive is the paused state, and it is now visible and recallable. Every pause, reactivation, archive and restore writes a line in the client's activity, so "when did this pause, for how long, and who did it" has an answer. The Deactivate dialog offers Now or On a date; a dated pause lands on its own that day through exactly the same path as a manual one. Every page of a paused client carries a banner with the date and a Reactivate button, and a scheduled pause shows with a Cancel.

Work you were owed keeps coming. A task due before the pause date still gets delivered; everything after it freezes with the client. Tasks on a paused client say so when you open them.

The Locations page now suggests Business Profiles that a connected Google account already manages and that match the client's website, with a one-click add. Suggestions only, never automatic: one website can front locations the client did not hire you for. And Analytics and Search Console now link within minutes of activation rather than waiting for the nightly run.

For migrated partners: thousands of imported content pieces, Business Profile posts and link placements now read as published, with their live URL, everywhere the platform shows them.

## Behind the scenes

Every client gets a business profile classification (service area, ecommerce, and so on) at discovery submission and again at activation if it is still missing. That profile decides which setup steps appear, so an unclassified client was quietly missing its Business Profile steps.

The nightly Google linking job had been running out of time since the middle of the month because Business Profile sync ran first and ate the window. Linkers now run first, and profiles whose data permanently fails are set aside for a week instead of retried every hour.

New campaigns are matched to link networks at activation, so the first link placement no longer waits on a hand pick. Migrated members who never signed in can be sent a branded set-your-password email, once. And we wrote down how we test agents: run against a real campaign, read the whole transcript, fix between reps.
