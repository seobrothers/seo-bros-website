The first day of the week went to the approval loop and to what agents read before they act.

## For our partners

Rank tracking follows the client's website. When a client's primary website changes, say from a subdomain to the root domain, the tracked keywords retarget with their ranking history intact, and an hourly check catches any that drifted.

When you send a task or a draft back with feedback, an agent takes the rework whichever way the task was created, and the notification says so. On the review page a send-back now opens its full feedback in a side sheet, with an Actioned badge once a new version has been submitted and Waiting until then. The thread has become History and sits under the decision, and the notes panel is the one place to write. The approval policy pair reads Ask first or Proceed without asking.

Requesting a discovery now includes picking the teammates on the prospect. They are assigned to the client on submit and are the people told when the report is ready. Owners and admins can also choose whether their client notifications cover assigned clients or every client.

## Behind the scenes

We reviewed the agent task feedback and the run transcripts behind it. The agent had been reading the page it was about to edit as truncated text, without looking at the rendered page. Agents now read a page's full HTML the way a search engine does and look at the render the way a visitor does before they may file an edit, and a read reports when it was served from a cache, because a cached page is what visitors are seeing.

Every campaign agent run now continues across sittings when the work runs long.

One partner's own rank tracker keeps most of a client's terms as history, never crawled. We stopped importing those and released them from the platform, and the agent's rank notes now say a term is kept as history instead of asking someone to escalate a registration problem.
