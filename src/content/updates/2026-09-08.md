Back from the long weekend with a day for the screens clients see.

## For our partners

Client Portal settings gain a Dashboard pages card: choose once which portal pages every client sees, and the per-client toggles on the Contacts tab still win when set. Three new portal options, all off by default: Campaign goal and Campaign brief show on the portal's ongoing phase in the same card your client overview uses, and Competitors shows the competitor sites as chips. An All phases switch, when off, drops the phase tabs from a client's Overview so they see only the current phase. The portal's ongoing phase also drops its planned-work list, so it matches your view.

Reporting Defaults is a new Workspace Settings tab: the same section toggles and PDF contents as the per-client sheet, inherited by every client. The per-client sheet stores only what differs, marks it Custom, and has a Use workspace defaults button.

Email Sending takes an optional reply-to address, applied to every client-facing send. A sending domain you have verified now goes live within the hour of its records landing, with no click.

Content plan items carry a real publish date: geo pages, service pages and page expansions land on the 15th of their month, and a plan's blog posts spread evenly across it, interleaving with posts from other plans. Writing opens on the 1st. The content plan table shows each item's type.

Proposals are now built from two catalogs, a campaign process outline and minimum monthly deliverables, and a won discovery carries both onto the campaign as its process and scope. Wording you wrote for a deliverable prints verbatim on the client's page.

## Behind the scenes

Every action staff take on held agent work is now recorded: edited (with what changed), rejected, released. The agent builder retired its always-on tool set; an agent's tool list is exactly what it names. More connector releases, this time from working the blog publishing lane by hand: posts open without a duplicate title heading, a featured image can lead the body for themes that never render it, a per-post index override, a permalink flush, reading settings for sites with no posts page, and new links dressed in the post's own link markup so readers can see them.
