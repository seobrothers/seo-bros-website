Friday brought one reporting change and a handful of smaller ones.

## For our partners

Conversions in the Website section of the report have always been the property's key events. Many partners never set those up, or have them tuned for Ads. The report settings sheet now has a Counted as conversions picker listing the events the linked property recorded over the last 90 days, busiest first, marked where they are already key events. Pick one or more and every conversion number in the report, the KPI, the daily chart and the By type list, uses that set in the partner view, the client portal, the CRM tab and the PDF. Nothing picked means key events, exactly as before.

Tasks are shaped by our team and read on your side; monitoring checks, content generation and campaign scope are set by our team. Each client's Contacts tab has a switch to pause all emails to that client, and Quick actions in the top bar pauses or resumes every client at once. Every discovery now gets an agent's draft followed by a strategist's review before you are told it is ready.

## Behind the scenes

Agents were briefly able to write facts. Alongside real ones, such as the company's history, came logs of what the agent had checked that day. An agent that must look at the site every time it works will always find something to write down, so the ability came out. Facts are written by people; agents read them and keep their notebooks.

Approving one draft had set the agent working on all four of the month's drafts at once, and it rewrote three it was never asked about. An agent sent to one task now touches that task only.

Page reads got more literal. A 404 is the site's own answer and counts as having read the page. Redirects are named hop by hop with their status, and a redirecting link is repointed rather than called dead. Domain monitoring stopped counting down to registration and certificate expiry, which flagged every live site once a year, and watches for a registration actually lapsing. Link placements survive a paused writer agent, and a placement that fails for a passing reason is tried again on the next pass.
