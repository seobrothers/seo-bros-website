A Tuesday for multi-location books and for the data agents read.

## For our partners

1. A brand groups clients that belong to one company: a franchise system, a dental service organisation, a roll-up. It carries context every agent working a member campaign reads, and rules every member campaign inherits.
2. The campaign's Rules & Context tab has a Campaign context card: lasting facts about a campaign, read by every agent and never shown to the client.
3. The Citations page shows notes per listing: what the listings service recorded (a directory login it created, a site it swapped for an alternative) alongside notes you or we add.
4. Notification settings offer Off, Weekly or Daily for the digest.
5. The send-back notice now says when the agent will pick it up, and review history shows comments in full, with long links wrapping inside the card.

### 1. Brands

A brand also carries the corporate website and an optional headquarters client. The Clients list has a Brand column, a filter, and a bulk action to add clients to a brand.

![The Clients list with a Brand column, a brand filter and a Brands button](/images/updates/2026-09-15/clients-brand-column.png)

Rules now come in three scopes, campaign, brand and workspace-wide, and a rule repeated across several member campaigns can be promoted into one brand rule.

![A brand page: details, brand context, the rules every member campaign follows, and its clients](/images/updates/2026-09-15/brand-page.png)

### 2. Campaign context

What the client's website platform will and will not allow is the kind of fact that belongs there. Notes are for things that change; context is for things that do not.

## Behind the scenes

The daily refresh of Search Console, Analytics, Ads and call tracking data now works through the connected properties one at a time, so every property refreshes each day however many clients we add.

When an agent reads a page's Search Console performance it now defaults to 90 days rather than 28. A quarter is what a strategist reads before moving a page; a month is a wobble. One agent had proposed retargeting a page away from its terms on two impressions in the 28-day window; the 90-day read showed hundreds.
